# Case case_008

## Scenario

- **Setting:** Cardiac rehabilitation clinic
- **Difficulty:** hard
- **Specialty:** cardiology
- **Nurse tasks:** Discuss return to driving/work, Explain new medications, Check understanding throughout
- **Patient:** Mr. Okafor, age 56, recovering from myocardial infarction, mood: shaken
- **Background:** Delivery driver, first heart attack, many practical and long-term questions.
- **Patient concerns/questions to raise:** When can I drive again?, Will I need this medication forever?
