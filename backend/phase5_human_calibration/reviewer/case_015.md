# Case case_015

## Scenario

- **Setting:** Orthopaedic ward
- **Difficulty:** medium
- **Specialty:** orthopaedic
- **Nurse tasks:** Explain recovery timeline, Address mobility concerns, Discuss pain management
- **Patient:** Sam, age 58, post-operative hip replacement, mood: anxious
- **Background:** First major surgery, lives alone, worried about independence.
- **Patient concerns/questions to raise:** Will I be able to walk again unaided?
